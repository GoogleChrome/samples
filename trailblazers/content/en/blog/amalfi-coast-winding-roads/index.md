![Amalfi, Italy](cover.jpg)

The Amalfi Coast is a vertical landscape where colorful houses cling to the cliffs like brightly colored barnacles. Being a bit of a history geek, I was less interested in the luxury beach clubs and more fascinated by the fact that this rugged coastline was once one of the most powerful maritime republics in the Mediterranean.

### Driving the Dream (and the Nightmare)

There is nothing quite like driving the SS163, the "Road of a Thousand Bends." With the scent of lemons blowing through the window and the salty Mediterranean air, it’s pure bliss—if you don't mind the white-knuckle adrenaline of a tour bus coming at you on a hairpin turn!

For a change of pace, we hiked the **Path of the Gods (Sentiero degli Dei)**. This trail high above the sea offers views that genuinely made me stop and catch my breath. We ended our day with a private **Positano boat tour**, looking back at the pyramid of pastel houses as the sun dipped below the horizon. Seeing Positano from the water is the only way to truly understand its scale.

### A Seafood Symphony

Dinner in Amalfi usually starts and ends with lemons. We sat at a tiny trattoria tucked away from the main piazza and had **Scialatielli ai frutti di mare**—thick, hand-rolled pasta served with a bounty of clams, mussels, and shrimp. And of course, no meal is complete without a chilled glass of local **Limoncello**. It’s liquid sunshine!

### Gotchas & Tips

- **Ditch the Car**: Unless you have nerves of steel, take the SITA bus or the ferry. Parking is nearly non-existent and incredibly expensive.
- **The "Steps" Tax**: Be prepared for steps. Thousands of them. If you have mobility issues, stay in Amalfi or Maiori rather than Positano.
- **Reservations are King**: If you want a table with a view for sunset, book at least 2-3 days in advance during the summer months.

The Amalfi Coast is a place of dramatic beauty and deep history. It’s a bit crowded, yes, but some things are famous for a reason.

### Highlights

- **Path of the Gods**: The best free view in Italy.
- **Positano from the water**: Pure cinematic magic.
- **Scialatielli**: The texture of the pasta is unforgettable.
- **Amalfi Cathedral**: A stunning blend of Arab-Norman architecture.
