![Banff, Canada](cover.jpg)

Banff is a place where the mountains don’t just loom; they embrace you. As someone who finds healing in nature, the Canadian Rockies felt like a massive outdoor cathedral. The air is so crisp it feels like it’s cleaning you from the inside out.

### Lakes of Impossible Blue

We started our journey **canoeing on Lake Louise**. The water is a turquoise so bright it looks like it’s been touched by a highlighter—an effect caused by "rock flour" from the melting glaciers. It’s incredibly serene to paddle out to the center of the lake and feel the silence of the surrounding peaks.

Afterward, we took the "workout with a reward" hike up to the **Agnes Lake Tea House**. It’s a steady climb, but sitting at an alpine lake with a hot cup of tea and a fresh scone while mountain goats wander nearby is pure magic. We ended our trip by soaking our sore muscles in the **Banff Upper Hot Springs**, watching the steam rise into the cool mountain air as the stars came out.

### Canadian Comforts

When in Canada, you have to embrace the comfort food. We found a small spot in town for a massive bowl of **Poutine**—crispy fries, squeaky cheese curds, and rich gravy. It’s the ultimate hiker's fuel. Of course, no trip to Banff is complete without a **BeaverTail**—a fried dough pastry shaped like a beaver’s tail, topped with cinnamon and sugar. It’s sweet, messy, and absolutely delicious.

### Gotchas & Tips

- **The Early Bird Gets the Lake**: If you want to see Moraine Lake or Lake Louise without 5,000 friends, you need to be there by 6:00 AM (or better yet, take the shuttle). The parking lots fill up before the sun is even fully up.
- **Bear Awareness**: This is grizzly territory. Always carry bear spray (and know how to use it), and make noise while you hike.
- **Dress in Layers**: Even in summer, the temperatures can drop to near-freezing at night.

### Highlights

- **Canoeing Lake Louise**: A bucket-list experience for a reason.
- **Agnes Lake Tea House**: The best view you’ll ever have with a cup of tea.
- **Banff Hot Springs**: The perfect way to end a day of adventure.
- **The Wildlife**: Seeing elk wandering through the town center is a daily occurrence here.

Banff is a place that reminds you of the scale of the world. It’s a landscape that invites you to be still and just wonder.
